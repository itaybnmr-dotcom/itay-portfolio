Record Day Branding

Motion posters for a fictional vinyl festival at the Teder, built on the material of records: lines, grain and handling.

Course: Moving Posters course, Third-year (2026) Mentor: Amitay Gilad.

Motion / Branding / Poster / IMG-Making
Designed and moved in After Effects, Indesign

The project centers on the material of records.

A lined texture refers to the grooves and striped sleeves, with grain.

Early versions were too abstract, so I visited record bars and a collector’s shop to watch how people handle records: pulling them from the sleeve, browsing, placing the needle. These actions appear as found footage, processed into the same texture.

Style & Motion

The animation runs at 12 fps for the feel of old film, and the type moves without smooth easing.

A circle, an abstracted record, moves top to bottom inside a square mask. The type is Secolo 22, narrowed, with Narkiss Block; running text works as image, while information stays readable. One color: deep green on worn cream.

​

The system stays fixed across five posters; the amount of information changes.

The bus poster balances mood and details, the schedule carries all the information with less motion, and three stories add found footage. Next, I’d test the brand on signage.
